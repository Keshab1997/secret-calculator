# Privacy, Safety & Acceptable Use Policy

**Effective date: 12 October 2026**

## What this app is

Calculator includes an optional private messaging area. Use it only with people you know and trust. A disguised interface does not make unlawful activity lawful and does not guarantee anonymity.

## Information handled

The app may process:

- an anonymous Firebase account ID and Personal ID;
- profile name, photo and about text;
- pairing and contact records;
- messages, photos, voice notes and supported call records;
- presence, last-seen, delivery and notification information;
- Firebase Cloud Messaging tokens; and
- basic app/device crash diagnostics through Firebase Crashlytics.

Calculator history, preferences, passcodes and encryption keys are also stored locally on the device.

## How information is used and shared

Information is used to pair contacts, deliver messages, media, calls and disguised notifications, remember settings, and diagnose crashes. Firebase and related Google services process cloud data needed to provide these features. A profile and conversation content are shared with contacts the user chooses to pair with. The app does not provide a public people directory.

## Encryption and its limits

Message text and supported media are encrypted on the device before upload, and room keys are kept in protected device storage. Some metadata—such as account, pairing, delivery, presence and notification information—is not message content and may be visible to service providers.

First contact is not cryptographically authenticated against a trusted identity. Users should verify a contact's Personal ID through another trusted channel. Encryption cannot protect content on a compromised or unlocked device, or content copied, photographed or disclosed by a recipient.

## Permissions

Camera/photos, microphone, notifications and biometric or device authentication are requested only for the feature the user chooses to use. Permissions can be denied or revoked through phone settings, although the related feature may stop working.

## Retention and deletion

Local app data remains until it is cleared or the app is uninstalled. Cloud records may remain according to conversation settings and the deployed Firebase configuration. Unpairing removes a contact from the device but may not delete shared history, backups, server records, or copies already saved by a recipient. Users should not send information that requires guaranteed erasure.

## Safety and acceptable use

Do not use this app for harassment, stalking, threats, fraud, impersonation, unauthorized access, malware, trafficking, exploitation, non-consensual intimate content, child sexual abuse material, planning violence, hiding evidence, or any other illegal or harmful activity. Do not contact or record people without any consent required by law. Each user is responsible for their messages and for complying with local law.

## Children

This app is not intended for children under 13. Anyone below the digital-consent age in their country should not use the messaging features without a parent or legal guardian.

## User choices and incident response

Users can decline permissions, avoid pairing, remove local profile information, unpair contacts, clear conversations where supported, or uninstall the app. A recipient may retain their own copy.

Anyone receiving harmful or illegal content should stop engaging, preserve evidence when safe, block contact outside the app, and report the matter to the appropriate platform or local authority. Policy questions and data requests can be submitted through the repository's support/Issues channel.

## Changes

This notice may change when app features or service providers change. The effective date above identifies the current version.
